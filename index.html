Where it comes from

You were twelve when your father was killed during the 1976 Soweto uprising. He is remembered as one of its unsung heroes. That loss shaped your life, and it put one question at the centre of your work: what do we carry, and what do we pass on?

You have spent more than fifteen years helping other people answer it, in boardrooms through nowhere Africa and YPO, and in constellation circles, retreats and private sessions. From the outside these look like separate businesses. They are one body of work. A leadership team stuck in an old pattern and a family repeating the same story for three generations need the same thing: someone who can help them see it.

Until now, each platform and your website has shown a different part of you. This strategy brings the parts together, so people meet one person, one story and clear ways to work with you.

Why enquiries, not followers

Followers and views are tracked, but they are leading indicators. A thousand new followers who never ask about a retreat do not help your business. An enquiry is where social, the website and operations all have to work together, so it tells us whether the whole ecosystem is doing its job.

Audience

Four groups, each arriving through a different door. All four respond to the same message: honest talk about family, purpose and what gets passed down. Only the format and the next step change.

The woman in her second half Core · 45 to 65

South African women, already the majority of your Instagram and Facebook. Often at a turning point: children leaving, a parent ageing, a career winding down. Asking what she is still carrying from her family.

Door: Instagram, Facebook, word of mouth. First step: a constellation group or the cards. Buys: retreats, constellations, the book.

The searcher Growth · 25 to 44

Mostly women reached through TikTok, arriving via searches about retreats and letting go of the past. Noticing patterns she would rather not repeat.

Door: TikTok search, reels. First step: saving content, the lead magnet, the cards. Buys: workshops, cards, constellations later.

The leader High value

Executives and business owners in your LinkedIn, nowhere and YPO networks. Successful and asking what it is all for. Responds to purpose retreats, as your two best LinkedIn posts showed.

Door: LinkedIn, referral. First step: a conversation. Buys: purpose retreats, team workshops, consulting.

Sample post
The retreat room
The most successful people I work with often arrive at a retreat with the same sentence. "I have everything I said I wanted. So why does it feel like something is missing?" It is not a crisis. Most of them are doing well by every measure that shows up in a performance review. What they have lost is the thread between what they do every day and why they started. A purpose retreat is not a holiday from that question. It is time set aside to answer it properly, away from the meetings and the phone, with people who are asking the same thing. On the first morning I ask everyone one question: what are you carrying that you never chose to pick up? The answers are rarely about work. They are about a parent's expectations, a family's rules about success, a promise made at 20 that no longer fits at 50. By the last day, most people have put something down. Some leave with a decision. Some leave with a better question. Almost everyone leaves lighter. If you have been asking yourself that sentence, I would like to hear from you. Website strategy

Your website is the one place you fully own, and the hub that everything on social leads to. This section sets out what it needs to look like, how people move through it, and why. It becomes Jonny's brief after sign-off.

The path through the site

1A post, a DM or a Google search
2Start here, or straight to an offer page
3The offer page answers their questions
4Enquire, book, WhatsApp or buy
5Brett replies the same day

Every page is built to move someone one step along this path. Anything that does not help them move along it is left out.

What it needs to look like, and why

Say who you are in five seconds

A headline, one sentence and one button above the fold, over a still of you.

Why: the current hero is a three-minute video with no words. Most visitors decide within seconds whether a page is for them.

Lead with your face

Your photographs and short videos throughout, not stock footage or strangers.

Why: people book this kind of work from a person they trust. Your face is the same thread that runs through social.

One page per offer

Retreats, Constellations, Workshops, Sessions and Cards each get a full page with their own words.

Why: people arrive looking for one thing. Separate pages also rank separately on Google. The current Retreats page has no words at all.

Every page ends with a next step

A short form, a WhatsApp button, a booking option or a list to join.

Why: the Constellations page currently ends straight into the footer, so interested people have nowhere to go.

Calm, warm and easy to read

The new brand, generous spacing, large text, plain language.

Why: most of your audience is over 45 and reading on a phone. Calm design also matches the feeling of the work.

Built for phones first

Designed on a phone screen first, loading in under three seconds.

Why: nearly all traffic from social arrives on a phone, and slow pages lose people before they load.

One voice throughout

The personal growth voice on every page, with consulting on its own page.

Why: the current footer tells retreat visitors they are on a leadership consultancy site.

Measurable from day one

GA4, a working pixel and tagged links.

Why: without them we cannot count enquiries, which is the north star.
